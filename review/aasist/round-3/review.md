## 3. Per scene (one line each)

- **a_intro (0–54.8 s):** The viewer learns that AASIST is a small (447 K) graph-attention back-end on top of XLS-R, with a spectral view, a temporal view and a master node, and meets the HUD and the F/N colours. Biggest gap: 1.1–5.7 s narrates XLS-R context vectors over a teaser labelled "inside AASIST: 42 rows × 67 frames". The "67 frames" label also clashes with the 201 frames used everywhere else (67 are pooled steps, 3 frames each).
- **a_overview (0–74 s):** Shows the 8-stage pipeline with a shape under each card. Biggest gap: 61.7–68.4 s, where "447 thousand" is spoken while only the 315,438,720 XLS-R bar shows. The "2 branches, 2 HS-GAL / 16 + 10 + 1" card is jargon at this point (and could be read as 2 HS-GAL layers when there are 4), and about 60 % of every frame is empty.
- **a_start (0–74.9 s):** Shows 64,600 samples → ⌊(64600−400)/320⌋+1 = 201 frames → LL 1024→128, with "frame 7 stays frame 7". Biggest gap: 60.6–74.7 s, a 14.5 s static fine-tuning box while two sentences of narration pass.
- **a_image (0–91.8 s):** Covers transpose → unsqueeze → 3×3 max pool (worked 6×6 example, all four maxima correct) → (B,1,42,67), then BN and SELU with a curve. Biggest gap: at 3.4→7.8 s the transposed 128 axis changes from orange to teal. 79.5–90.7 s holds the finished SELU curve for about 10 s.
- **a_conv2d (0–73.2 s):** A 2-D convolution with real numbers (all checked outputs correct), weight (32 out, 32 in, 2, 3) with 6,176 numbers, and padding (1,1)/(0,1) that keeps 42×67. Biggest gap: at 61 s the conv2 input is a fresh random grid rather than conv1's 5×7 output, so the two-step story breaks. The stride s in the formula is never given a value.
- **a_res (0–76 s):** The residual block, the 6-block channel table (1→32→32→64→64→64→64, skip 1×3 on blocks 1 and 3), the code quirk, and 211,072 params = 47 %. Biggest gap: the block is only a flowchart with no tensor picture of the skip addition, and the code-quirk aside (39–59 s) is 20 s of text.
- **a_nodes (0–83.6 s):** The attention map w, softmax over T with a weighted sum giving 42 spectral nodes plus pos_S, and softmax over F giving 67 temporal nodes. Clearest scene so far. Biggest gap: at 60.5–62.7 s the temporal transpose just appears, and the original-vs-SSL comparison (69–82 s) is two text boxes.
- **a_gat (0–87.4 s):** Fully connected nodes → x_i ⊙ x_j (B,42,42,64) → tanh(Linear)·a → 42×42 scores → softmax(score/T) along node 3's row (weights 0.12 … 0.26 sum to 1.00) → W₁(A·X)+W₂X → BN, SELU. The best scene. Biggest gap: the i/j axis labels on the maps are single faint glyphs, "T" means temperature here but time elsewhere (softmax_T in a_nodes), and 77–86 s is a two-card hold for GAT_layer_T.
- **a_pool (0–44.2 s):** Sigmoid score, keep the top ⌊N·0.5⌋, multiply by the score, 42→21, 67→33, sorted by score. Biggest gap: at 16.6 s the score labels overlap during the re-sort, and the 14-node toy row is never said to be a stand-in, or which set it is.
- **a_hsgal (0–76.8 s):** Type projections, stacking 33+21 = 54, a 54×54 map with three scoring vectors, temperature 100, a master node, the projection to 32, then the split into (B,33,32)/(B,21,32)/(B,1,32). Biggest gap: the master-node rule is a grey caption shown under its reading time, α is undefined, and "32" in "att_weight (32 → 1)" is only explained by a faint caption.
- **a_branch (0–47.2 s):** Pool to 16/10, a second HS-GAL with residual, two branches with separate masters, element-wise max (worked example correct). Biggest gap: it is only a flowchart. The master's residual and max are drawn dim, and 39.5–47 s is a hold.
- **a_readout (0–66.7 s):** max|·| and mean over nodes for T and S, plus the master → 160 → dropout → Linear → (B,2), with softmax 0.12/0.88 correct for the logits shown. Biggest gap: 38.9–52.3 s dumps the training config in one breath, and the "node counts never reach the classifier" point comes after the training detour instead of right after the concat.
- **a_orig (0–74.9 s):** Sinc conv 70×129 → (B,70,64472) → pool (B,1,23,21490) → time shrinks to 29 → 23+29 nodes → 11+20, with 297,866 params. Biggest gap: the pooled thumbnail has no axis labels and the size-1 channel axis appears unexplained. The 7-row comparison table is dense text.
- **a_code (0–43.5 s):** The authors'-code trace matches every shape derived earlier. Biggest gap: plain text that builds slowly (5.7–17.2 s shows one line), with a very dim header line.
- **a_recap (0–76.6 s):** A one-line-per-step table, then the key-idea diagram. Biggest gap: the table sits in the top third in small type with the HUD removed, and 5.7–14.5 s shows one row.

## 5. Ten worst problems (worst first) with concrete fixes

1. **a_start 60.6–74.7 s: 14.5 s dead stretch (E8).** The "XLS-R + back-end trained end-to-end · Adam, lr = 1e-6" box sits unchanged through two sentences. *Fix:* cut the hold to ≤ 4 s, or animate the idea. Pulse the dashed red gradient arrow into the XLS-R grid on "tiny steps", and on "destroyed early in training" show a small side-by-side: lr 1e-6, where the feature grid barely changes, against a large lr, where the grid scrambles.
2. **a_readout 38.9–52.3 s: training config dumped in one breath (E4 + E5).** It packs 7 new items into about 13 s as a text list, with no reason given for 0.1/0.9 and RawBoost unexplained. *Fix:* split it into two beats. (a) Add a two-bar class-count chart (spoof ≫ bona fide) so the 0.9 weight visibly evens it out. (b) Drop "batch 14" from the narration and leave it as a footnote. If RawBoost stays, show a 1-s waveform before/after (filtered + noise). Otherwise just name it as "data augmentation" without the term.
3. **a_overview 61.7–68.4 s: parameter comparison out of sync (E1).** *Fix:* on "four hundred and forty-seven thousand" (61.7 s), show the `AASIST back-end 447,242` sliver alone. Then grow the full-width `XLS-R 315,438,720` bar on "three hundred and fifteen million" (68 s), so the ratio lands when it is spoken.
4. **a_hsgal ≈ 50.8–58 s: the master-node rule lives only in a too-brief grey caption, and α is undefined (E4 + E5).** *Fix:* narrate "the master reads every node, but no node reads the master". Draw the 54 edges as one-way arrows pointing into the star, and hold ≥ 7 s. Rewrite the formula as `new master = W(Σⱼ αⱼ xⱼ) + W′(master)` with "αⱼ = master's attention weights (sum to 1)" written beneath it at full brightness.
5. **a_pool 14.4–18.8 s: overlapping score labels during the top-k re-sort (E7).** *Fix:* do it in two moves. First fade the 7 losers out and close the gaps in place. Then re-order with each node lifted on its own small arc, or hide the labels during the move and fade them back in once the order "0.75 0.72 0.66 …" has settled. Add "14 nodes shown · real layer: 42 spectral / 67 temporal".
6. **a_image 3.4–7.8 s (and from a_start 36 s): the feature axis changes colour mid-transpose.** 128 is orange in (B, 201, 128) and teal in (B, 128, 201), and orange is then reused for the channel axis C. *Fix:* colour the feature axis teal (F) from LL's output onward, `(B, 201, 128)` with 128 teal, so the transpose visibly moves the same teal axis. Keep orange for channels only.
7. **Long holds elsewhere (E8):** `a_image` 78.5–88.5 s, `a_recap` 5.7–14.5 s, `a_readout` 58.1–66.6 s, `a_conv2d` 64.5–73 s, `a_overview` 5.2–13.2 s, `a_gat` 80–87 s. *Fix:* trim trailing silences to ≤ 3 s. In the recap, add rows on the beat of each sentence. In `a_gat` 77–86 s, replace the two text cards with the 67×67 temporal weight map next to the 42×42 one.
8. **a_gat 34.7–59.4 s: unreadable map axes and a symbol clash.** "i" and "j" are single faint glyphs (about 12 px at 1080p) with no node numbers, and the temperature is written "T", the letter used for the time axis (`softmax_T(w)` in a_nodes 80 s). *Fix:* label rows "i (node being updated) 1…8" and columns "j (neighbour) 1…8" at ≥ 20 px. Write `softmax(score / τ)` and say "temperature τ = 2".
9. **a_hsgal 28.2–48.5 s: "att_weight (32 → 1)" appears before the viewer knows pairs are 32-wide.** The 64→32 projection is only a faint caption at 39.5 s, and the 54×54 map's columns are unlabelled. *Fix:* put the chain `pairs (B,54,54,64) → tanh(Linear 64→32) → (B,54,54,32) → · att_weight → (B,54,54,1)` at full brightness above the map, and put it in the HUD. Label the columns "33 T | 21 S" to match the rows.
10. **Continuity breaks between steps (Motion).** In `a_conv2d` 61.0 s the conv2 input is a new random grid instead of conv1's 5×7 output. In `a_hsgal` 16.9→28.2 s the stacked node row fades out instead of becoming the 54×54 map's row and column headers. Many beats fade to an empty frame first (`motion-03` b4, `motion-09` b4, `motion-12` b3). *Fix:* slide conv1's output grid into the input slot, then add side zeros. Fly the stacked T/S nodes to the map's left and top edges as its headers. Crossfade beats directly instead of through black-empty frames.

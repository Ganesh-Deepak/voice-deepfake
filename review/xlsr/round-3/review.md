## 4. Factual check

Everything checked against the given facts is correct. Width 1024; 24 pre-norm layers; 16 heads × 64; FFN 4096; 512 conv channels with a LayerNorm after every conv (encoder 147.5 s, recap 10 s); 1 s = 16,000 samples = 49 frames; 4 s crop = 64,600 samples = 201 frames (deepfake labels it "≈ 4.04 s", which is accurate); 25 hidden states; 315,438,720 parameters.

These independently checked numbers are also correct: the conv toy outputs (−0.7, 1.2, −0.1, −0.9) and L_out = 4; the lengths 3199/1599/799/399/199/99/49; receptive field 400 and stride 320; 786,432; 525,312; posconv padding 177 → 50 → 49, 134.2 M → 8.39 M; the attention toy (scores 3.56/3.53/0.92/−0.25, ÷1.41, softmax 0.46/0.44/0.07/0.03, out [1.23, 0.45]); 4,198,400 + 8,393,728 + 4,096 = 12,596,224; × 24 = 302,309,376 (96 %); 2,401 / 40,401; 1 − 0.935¹⁰ ≈ 49 %; 26/49 = 53 %; 4.21 + 0.53 + 8.39 + 302.31 M; 320² = 102,400; 512→640, 384×2 = 768, 1024→768, 100 negatives, τ = 0.1, diversity 0.1; class weights [0.1, 0.9] with 0 = spoof; Linear 1024→128.

Errors and imprecisions:

1. **outputs 3–62 s (and the stack legend): h₂₄ is mislabelled.** The video labels "h₁ … h₂₄ = output of layers 1 … 24" and "last_hidden_state = LayerNorm(h₂₄)". In Hugging Face's stable-layer-norm encoder (used by XLS-R), the 25th entry of `out.hidden_states` is appended *after* the final LayerNorm. So `hidden_states[24]` **is** `last_hidden_state`, and the raw layer-24 output is never returned. A viewer who runs `torch.equal(out.hidden_states[-1], out.last_hidden_state)` gets `True`, which contradicts the slide. This also matters for the deepfake layer-weighting, since one of the 25 inputs is normalised and the others are not.
2. **attn 54–66 s (minor)**: the label says "wav2vec 2.0 heads: d = 64 → ÷8" while the voice says "XLS-R's attention heads". Use "XLS-R heads" to match.
3. **outputs 48–62 s (claim, not an error)**: "layer-wise studies find the lower layers most discriminative" has no citation on screen; add a source tag or soften to "lower-to-middle layers".
4. **intro 21–25 s (cosmetic)**: the count-up animations show wrong interim values (205.4 M, 956, 433 K) while the narration states the real figures. Finish the count before the number is spoken, or skip the count-up.

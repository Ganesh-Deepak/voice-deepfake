Times are **scene-local seconds** as printed on the storyboard frames; global `mm:ss` is given where it helps the editor. Chapter-card flashes ("CHAPTER NN / 17", docking title words) were ignored, as instructed.

**Verdict: 62 / 100: rework the failing scenes.** 8 of 9 defects fail, so every dimension is capped at 6. The teaching core is strong: conv from scratch, the encoder length formula, projection, the toy attention example and the MHA shape walk all hold up. A broken render in `x_layer` stops it from shipping. So do a label in `posconv` whose meaning is inverted, a transpose that is never actually shown, and long static stretches.

## 4. Factual / number check

The required numbers are all correct wherever they appear: 1024 hidden, 24 layers, 16 × 64 heads, FFN 4096, 512 conv channels, 16,000 → 49, 64,600 → 201, 25 hidden states, 315,438,720 ≈ 315.4 M. I checked the arithmetic too: 3,199/1,599/799/399/199/99/49, 5·2⁶ = 320, RF = 400, 786,432, 525,312, 134.2 M / 8.39 M, 4,198,400, 8,393,728, 12,596,224, ×24 = 302.31 M, 2,401 / 40,401, the toy attention values, and the softmax 0.07 / 0.93. **No leftover 768/3072/12-head/13-state/94 M values.** The 768s in `quant` and `contrast` are correct (codevector / projection dim of XLS-R).

Issues:
1. **posconv 79–117 s:** the visible label reads "LayerNorm here (pre-norm model)" because the ⊕ hides "no". As rendered, it states the opposite of the narration and the model.
2. **outputs 23–60 s:** "h₁ … h₂₄ = raw output of layers 1 … 24", with narration "plus the raw output of each of the twenty-four layers". In Hugging Face's `Wav2Vec2EncoderStableLayerNorm`, the 25th tuple entry is taken *after* the final encoder LayerNorm, so it equals `last_hidden_state`, not the raw layer-24 output. Reword to "h₁…h₂₃ raw, h₂₄ = after final LayerNorm", or verify with the repo's verify script.
3. **quant 56–67 s:** "temperature 2 → 0.5". The wav2vec 2.0 paper anneals to 0.5 for BASE and **0.1 for LARGE**, and XLS-R follows the LARGE recipe. Check the XLS-R config before keeping 0.5.
4. **projection 23–32 s:** the screen says "≈ 25 ms of audio" while the voice says "twenty-millisecond moment". Both are defensible (25 ms window, 20 ms hop), but they contradict each other at the same moment. Write "25 ms window, one every 20 ms".
5. **deepfake 3–10 s:** the box is labelled "wav2vec 2.0 + classifier head" in an XLS-R episode. Label it "XLS-R (wav2vec 2.0 arch.)".
6. **recap formula line:** "66800 → 208" is arithmetically right but unexplained. Name the detector that uses it, or drop it.

## 5. Ten worst problems (worst first)

1. **x_layer 83.7–102.6 s (21:20–21:39). Broken render.** Red `ERR X.card is not a function` sits on the diagram for 18 s and runs into the cut (`cut-12`). The 24-layer stack, the "302 M / 96 %" payoff and "flows through all twenty-four without changing shape" never appear. **Fix:** fix the card builder. Animate 24 layer cards stacking onto the residual stream with a running counter "12.6 M × 24 = 302.3 M (96 %)", and pulse (B,49,1024) at top and bottom. Add a build check that fails on any rendered text beginning `ERR`.
2. **posconv 79.3–117.2 s (15:23–16:01). Label meaning inverted.** The ⊕ node covers "no", so the screen says "LayerNorm here (pre-norm model)" while the narration says there is none. 84–106 s is also a 22 s static hold. **Fix:** put the label ≥ 16 px right of the ⊕ and rewrite it as "no LayerNorm here — it lives inside each layer (pre-norm)". Draw a dashed, struck-through LayerNorm box at 90 s when the sentence starts, then cut to the next idea by 100 s.
3. **posconv 34.6–56.9 s (14:38–15:01). The transposes are not shown.** (B,49,1024) → (B,1024,49) → (B,1024,50) → trim → (B,49,1024) is spoken. On screen the grid keeps the same orientation and the chain is a ~15 px grey line. **Fix:** flip the grid exactly as in `projection` 5–10 s, with the axis labels swapping (time ↔ 1024). Write each shape full size in the colour tuple style. Show the 50th output column turning red and sliding out on "trimmed off".
4. **encoder 45.5–54 s (8:38–8:46). Rushed and mismatched.** Five formula results (799, 399, 199, 99, 49) land in ~7 s while the narration says only "One ninety-nine.", spoken over the 399 frame. **Fix:** restore the narration ("seven ninety-nine, three ninety-nine, one ninety-nine, ninety-nine, forty-nine") and hold each formula ≥ 2 s. 5. **conv 78–111 s (6:45–7:18). A 33 s static stage running ahead of the voice.** The code panel (B,1,16000) → (B,512,3199), weight (512,1,10) and the HUD "after conv 1" all appear at 78 s. Keep the HUD at B·1·16000 until 94 s.

7. **conv 23.2–36.4 s (5:50–6:03).

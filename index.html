How the numbers were checked

Every shape and parameter count on screen was traced in PyTorch: Hugging Face Wav2Vec2Model built from the XLS-R 300M config, the SSL-AASIST authors' back-end, and a CPU replica of the official XLSR-MamBo code. The scripts are in the repository's verify/ folder.

Making of

Deterministic canvas animation, narrated with Kokoro (af_heart). Motion follows HyperFrames' motion doctrine and the motion-graphic skill's springs; music and sound effects are synthesized in tools/sound.py.
